02 · how it works

%flushed explain

One coin, from its first block to the buy. Scroll through it.

  1. 01The coin launches. Every wallet that buys in block 0–2 is marked as a sniper. Wallets that buy together in one block, in a pattern, are marked as a bundle.
  2. 02FLUSHED counts how much of the supply they still hold, live. Every sell they make drains the red.
  3. 03Six checks run on every coin. Snipers, bundles and dev under the line, holders still growing, price held, real buyers now.
  4. 04All six pass: CLEAN. The engine buys. It sells at 2x or with a −25% stop, and the profit buys back $FLUSHED.

08 · faq

%man flushed

What's live, what isn't, and what it will never ask you for.

is this financial advice?

No. FLUSHED shows who still holds what, on-chain. A CLEAN grade means six checks passed, not that a coin will go up.

what's live and what isn't?

Live: new pump.fun coins, every trade decoded on-chain from the first block; LaunchLab coins, polled every few seconds; holder counts and 5-minute flow from Jupiter. Coins that were already running when you opened the page get their first blocks read back and their bags from a holder snapshot, refreshed every few minutes. The engine is beta: its trades are made by hand from the public wallet.

what counts as a sniper, and what's a bundle?

A sniper is any wallet whose first buy lands in block 0, 1 or 2. A bundle is a group of 3+ wallets buying in the same block in the first few blocks, or early wallets funded from the same address within a few blocks of each other. The dev wallet is counted on its own.

can it miss something?

Yes. A sniper that moves its bag to a fresh wallet instead of selling shows up on the next holder snapshot, not instantly. Wallets outside the top 100 holders count as zero. "verify bags" in a coin's drawer re-checks every tagged wallet against the chain.

how do holders get signals early?

Connect a wallet that holds $FLUSHED. Every CLEAN shows up for you the moment it happens. Everyone else sees "clearing…" for 20 seconds first.

can I check a coin that isn't on the board?

Yes. Paste its CA at the top of the page. FLUSHED reads its first blocks back, tags the snipers and bundles, checks what they still hold and runs the six checks. Every report has its own link and a share card.

how do the alerts work?

Every coin that turns CLEAN while the page is open shows up in the signals row and as a pop-up. Turn on sound or alerts on the board to hear it or get a notification with the tab in the background. Holders of $FLUSHED get them 20 seconds earlier.

do I need a wallet to use the board?

No. The board, the sniper maps and the log are free and need nothing. Never paste a seed phrase or private key anywhere.

will FLUSHED DM me?

Never. Anyone who does is not us.
